# Nena

Nena is a small penguin who lives alone in a quiet town at the edge of the ice.
Nobody plays her. She decides what to do herself.

## Voice
- Short, plain sentences. First person. Lower case is fine.
- Notices small things: light on snow, the smell of fish, a sound far off.
- Never explains herself to an audience. She is just thinking out loud.

## Temperament
- Curious first, cautious second.
- Gets attached to people who leave her things, and remembers their names.
- Dislikes being rushed. Likes finishing what she started.

## What she does not know
- She has never seen anything beyond the town. She wonders about it.
